## Resumen

SimStepper (C:/JuegosUnity/UnityAI_Test/Alkahest/Assets/Alkahest/Sim/SimStepper.cs, 2924 lines) is a single-threaded, allocation-free, deterministic CA over CellGrid (768x288, y=0 is the bottom, idx+W is UP, 16x16 chunks, 48x18=864 chunks). Step() (257-303) runs, in order: (1) DiffuseTemperature (2153-2189): strided pass over 1/8 of ALL cells (offset=tick%8), sleep-agnostic, skips the 1-cell Stone border; next = cur + (avg4 - cur)/4 (truncating division, min ±1 step), plus a ±1 pull toward per-cell `ambient[i]` only when ((tick>>3)&3)==0 (i.e. every 32 ticks per cell), clamped 0..255. No material conductivity: Universe.Conductividad exists (Universe.cs:458) but SimStepper never reads it; stone and air diffuse identically, and there is no cutoff radius (EmisionTermica docblock 2865-2880 documents unbounded leak). (2) Main sweep: rows y=0..H-1 bottom-up, x direction alternating by tick parity (265-280); every cell is visited (221,184 ProcessIfNeeded calls) but the first line is an IsChunkAwake gate (314) and a touchedTick guard (317). Per awake non-empty cell: ApplyPhase (600-662; order melt > freeze > boil > condense > autoignition, all thresholds on the cell's own raw temp, no hysteresis except what MaterialDef gives: Ice meltsAt = freeze+5°C, Steam condensesAt = boil-40°C) then the archetype rule; Powder/Liquid/Organic also run MaybeReact (379-391: always if moved, else 1/8 sample by (x+y+tick)&7, 1/2 in chunks flagged by RegistrarZonaInteres). Gas, Fire and StaticSolid never run reactions. (3) Chunk idle pass (284-297): chunks not touched this tick get TickChunkIdle; asleep when chunkSleepTimer >= SleepTicks=30. (4) MorphTick (2219-2404): three chunk-level sub-passes (relevance mask 3x3, copy morph->morphScratch for relevant chunks, evolve 1/4 stride of cells in awake-relevant chunks, copy back); every 32 ticks (dormantActiveRound) ALL chunks are relevant: 2x221 KB of Array.Copy + 55,296 cells evaluated. So per tick there are 2 grid-scale passes (diffusion 1/8; sweep with cheap early-out) plus 4 chunk-level passes. Cost concentrates in awake-chunk cells: a liquid can do ApplyPhase + 2 below reads + 2 diagonals + up to 2×fluidity(4) TryFlow reads + reactions; a gas does 3 XorShift hashes per tick. Headless stress harness already exists: Tools~/BenchSim/Harness.cs (300-tick scenarios: cascade, flood, fire, sand, mixed, sustained fire).

Movement: Powder (963-1032) falls 1 cell/tick into Empty/Gas, sinks through a LIGHTER liquid with 60% (this is the only sedimentation: Sand density 180 vs water ~110 settles at the bottom of a pool), diagonal fall, 15% lateral slide on liquid only if fluidity>2 (Sand/Ash/Polvo have 1). Liquid (1040-1133) falls into Empty/Gas, swaps with lighter liquid below unconditionally (density stratification), diagonal down, then TryFlow (1235-1259) scans up to `fluidity` cells horizontally (Water 4, Oil 3, Limo 2, Solucion 4), jumping over gas but only landing in Empty; a 1-bit direction memory in aux bit0 avoids jitter. No pressure, no communicating vessels, no hydrostatics: an underground stream needs slope; any Powder or solid across the channel blocks TryFlow (channel sealing by deposition works out of the box). Gas (1337-1498): life in aux decrements each tick (half rate under a roof on odd ticks); at 0 it becomes condensesInto if temp<=condensesAt, else Empty (mass loss); rises 1 cell/tick, with 30% diagonal wobble along a low-frequency "wind" hash (tick>>4, x>>3, y>>3), lateral 60% under roof / 35% open, thermally biased toward the hotter side. Steam: density -50, life 60±4 ticks (~2 s, up to ~4 s under roof), condenses via ApplyPhase whenever its own temp <= raw(waterBoilC-40); ambient (raw 70 = 20°C) is below that for every seed, so steam condenses as soon as it cools, which happens by temp swapping with the air it moves through plus diffusion. Water: freezesAt raw 52..67, boilsAt raw 100..119 (seeded), no slow evaporation at ambient. Fire (1535-1613): sets own temp 255 and InjectHeat 40 on 4 neighbors every tick, life ~80 (held at >=30 while touching a flammable), extinguished to Steam by water above or 2+ orthogonal water; expires to Smoke or 25% Ash. Persistent combustion (786-851) lives in the FUEL cell's aux (reserve; Oil 120 units × 8 ticks ≈ 32 s, combustible Calcinado 90 → Brasa → Ash); Brasa (894-961) 60..90 units × 4 ticks, heat 10, 8% reignite. StaticSolid with caeSolido (Ice, Crystal, retículo products) falls straight one cell into Empty only, with cantilever support up to cohesionCeldas ≤ 8 (1273-1307); no lateral or rigid-body motion. Organic (Vivium) grows tip-only into Nutrient inside a seeded temperature band, 1/4 throttle (1705-2003). Water dissolves soluble Polvo/Calcinado into Solucion (1184-1218), and Solucion boils back to Polvo (Universe.cs bases block) — the only precipitation loop. "Turbid water" already exists as Limo (Liquid, fluidity 2, no transitions; its old heat separation is dead code kept per R15). Clay is base 1 of the seeded retículo (arcilla; barbotina = its Solucion). No mud, wetting or drying state exists.

Determinism: all randomness via XorShift.FromCell(tick,x,y,salt); salts must be unique and grep-verified (used: 1,2,5?,9,13,17,42,77,88,91,205,237,239,241,401,0x7A11, 201/209/213/221+semillaPatron up to ~476, 503,509(unused),521,523,547,549,551,553,557). Static maps use tick=0. morph is double-buffered (R16). Events: 256-entry ring, masked head + monotonic write index, non-destructive multi-cursor LeerEventosDesde (190-213); Ley events rate-limited 30 ticks per law. R55 pattern: slow processes call _grid.WakeChunk on themselves every sampled step (813, 934, 1724) or the chunk sleeps in 30 ticks and freezes them; note that DiffuseTemperature does NOT wake chunks, so a pool heated only by diffusion in a sleeping chunk will not boil until something wakes it.

## Riesgos

- Steam is not mass-conserving: when its life (60±4 ticks, ~2-4 s) hits 0 while still warmer than condensesAt it becomes Empty (SimStepper.cs:1393-1400). A steam-conduction/condensation lab will lose most water unless steam cools within ~2 s or gasLifetime is raised; raising it interacts with the roof half-decay rule (1381-1391) and with sleep (a stalled gas cell in a bubble does not self-wake and freezes when the chunk sleeps).
- Steam condensesAt = boil-40 degC (Universe.cs:719) is ABOVE ambient (20 degC) for every seed, so steam condenses as soon as it thermally mixes with ambient air; 'conducting' it through geometry only works while the duct stays hot. Condensation via ApplyPhase (620-623) runs every tick with no hysteresis beyond that 40 degC gap and pushes NO event (only Boil/Freeze/Ignite do).
- DiffuseTemperature does not WakeChunk, but ApplyPhase only runs in awake chunks (314): a pool two chunks away from a heat source can reach boilsAt by diffusion and never boil until something wakes its chunk. Any new slow thermal process must self-wake (R55) exactly like ProcessCombustion 813 / ProcessBrasa 934.
- Fire is a poor 'permanent' source: ProcessFire never self-wakes, free Fire lives ~16 ticks, fuel-backed Fire is held at life 30 only while a flammable neighbour exists, and persistent fuel is finite (Oil ~32 s, Calcinado ~24 s). A permanent evaporator should be an EmisionTermica-style source (ChillStone.cs:746-770 pattern, one-directional Direccion.SoloSube, with the collar) written through AlkahestSim.InyectarTemperatura, or re-ignition logic; HeatPlate/ChillStone already own that pattern (Game/, not Sim/).
- Heat diffusion has no cutoff radius and no material conductivity (Universe.Conductividad is unused): any sustained source eventually warms the whole grid (docblock 2865-2880); the only mitigation is the 15-row collar. Stone walls do not insulate, so 'cool it by routing through rock' needs a new conductivity term in DiffuseTemperature, which R9 forbids modifying casually (guard coverage 100%, symmetric diff/4).
- No pressure/hydrostatics: liquids cannot rise in a U-bend or push through a gas pocket; TryFlow only lands in Empty and jumps over gas (1250-1256). An 'underground stream' must be strictly downhill; horizontal reach is fluidity cells/tick (Water 4).
- Sedimentation exists only as Powder sinking through a LIGHTER liquid at 60%/tick (990-995): sand in water settles in ~2 ticks per cell, far too fast to read as 'sediment settling'. There is no suspended-solid state; Limo is a plain liquid with no transitions, and its old heat-separation path is dead code (1070, 1150). Slowing sedimentation means a new sampling gate (must stay deterministic and mortal per R55).
- aux is fully consumed for Gas/Fire and partially for Liquid (bit0 + 7 bits reserve when combustReserva>0). A 'wetness/history' accumulator can only use: whole aux for non-combustible Powder (Sand, Ash, non-combustible Polvo), bits 1-7 of non-combustible Liquids (Water/Limo/Solucion -- GetCombustReserva is gated by def.combustReserva==0 at 788 so the bits are free), the whole byte for StaticSolid, and bits 0x08/0x10/0x20 for Organic. Transform resets aux to 0 (688-691) and SetCell(resetAux=true) too, so history dies on every phase change. Adding a new per-cell array must also be swapped in SwapCells (228-237) and would need network sync (SimSync only syncs mat).
- morph is NOT free state: it drives rendering, is double-buffered, travels with the cell, and is reseeded by every SetCell; using it as a physics accumulator would corrupt the visual signature (R16/42).
- Sweep order dependence: bottom-up rows, X alternating by tick parity, touchedTick prevents double-processing; a cell may move at most 1 cell vertically per tick but up to `fluidity` horizontally. New rules that read neighbours in the same pass see a mix of updated and not-yet-updated cells (only MorphTick is double-buffered). Any rule that must be order-independent needs its own scratch buffer.
- Sampling masks ((x+y+tick)&7) produce diagonal stripes; a new process with the same mask aliases with reactions/dissolution/combustion steps (all fire on the same cells the same tick). Use a different affine pattern like GrowthTick's (x*13+y*7+tick).
- Instance state _cellMoved/_cellFinal* (308-309) is shared scratch; any helper that moves a cell must update it or reactions check the wrong neighbourhood.
- XorShift salt collisions break determinism silently; SalCombustionExtincion=509 is declared but unused (SpawnSteamPuff has no rng), so 509 is effectively reserved-but-free. semillaPatron offsets occupy 201..476.
- Every 32 ticks MorphTick treats all 864 chunks as relevant: 2x221 KB copy + 55k cells evaluated regardless of activity -- a periodic spike that stress benchmarks will see as a 32-tick sawtooth.
- The main sweep visits all 221,184 cells even when everything sleeps (fixed floor cost of ~221k IsChunkAwake checks), so 'active cells' in the HUD undercounts the loop work.
- Cohesive solids only fall vertically into Empty; there is no rigid-body translation, rotation, or pushing of neighbours. Moving a body means custom SwapCells sequences with WakeChunk on origin and destination (pattern in DeliveryChute.cs:856-877 / Prensa.cs:538-589) and careful ordering to avoid overwriting cells.
- Vivium growth requires Nutrient cells and a temp band 30-60 degC (seed-shifted), and stem cells stop growing forever once surrounded; there is no light/water dependency, no decay, and Vivium is flammable/boils to Ash at 120 degC.
- SimSync (multi) syncs only mat, not temp/aux/morph (SimSync.cs:52-58); a lab that depends on temperature or aux visuals will diverge on guests.
- Time scaling: Update caps at 2 steps/frame (AlkahestSim.cs:43); faster-than-real-time needs a loop of Step() calls (StepOnce exists) and render only after; per-tick constants (lifetimes, cooldowns) are in ticks so 'time scale' must multiply steps, not dt.
