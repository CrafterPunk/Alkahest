## Resumen

All 15 documents (repo root C:/JuegosUnity/UnityAI_Test/Alkahest; paths below are relative to it) were read in full. They are design/contract archives from playtests 21-95 of a deterministic 768x288 @30Hz falling-sand engine. Chronology relevant to a "laboratory" gallery:

1) PIVOT_LABORATORIO_VIVO (pt21): "the big workshop stays, buried in bedrock" — intimate carved room (BuildCuartoIntimo), no clock, HUD silenced, Criatura (Vivium organic body, heartbeat, hunger, digestion into seed-affine products) + Capullo (heat-driven incubation). Adenda "HERRAMIENTAS VIVAS": creatures with continuous thermal temperament replacing HeatPlate/ChillStone, offspring inherit with deviation; decided but never implemented (sandbox resets lost it). The pivot itself was built and played (pt21-23).

2) CONTRATO_MAREA (pt24): dark tide liquid (density 200, fluidity ~120) rising from a basement heart, converting materials by 1/8-sampled per-cell process (not a ReactionEngine law), cured by Rocío exuded by the creature. Built, played, then discarded in pt25 as a direction (DISENO_LO_QUE_PERSISTE §8.7, CONTRATO_PERSISTE §1 says any Marea/Rocio remnants are errors). No detailed "why" beyond "descartada como dirección"; the pattern (gated per-cell process with own salts) is the reusable part.

3) DISENO/CONTRATO_PERSISTE (pt25): the biggest reorganization. Limo primigenio (id 17, turbid liquid, fluidity 2) drips from a fissure; 5 bases x 8 states lattice (ids 18..57), Crisol by fuel tier (tier0 raw 118 boils water/limo, never melts), Prensa, Banco de chispa, Columna de ensayo (5 wide x 22 tall glass tube for density stratification), solver guaranteeing every seed has a persistent winner and a combustible calcined base. Two new SimStepper processes only: water+soluble powder -> Solucion (20%/sample, salt 237) and Limo at temp>=raw150 -> powder chosen deterministically per cell (salt 239). Explicitly deferred/vetoed: pressure as a field (never), cold fragility (v2), electricity in world, multi-solute concentrations, automation, organisms (Criatura/Capullo parked intact), Marea (retired).

4) INFORME_MOTOR (pt38) is the only doc with measured performance: headless harness (Tools~/BenchSim/Harness.cs), 300 ticks after 30 warmup: Cascada 2.29 ms mean / 5.86 peak (8k active cells); Diluvio (half world water) 5.48 / 11.65 (74k cells); Incendio 2.49 / 4.15 (17.9k); Arena masiva 1.99 / 2.97 (15k); Mixto 3.94 / 5.80 (41.6k). In-game F3: 0.6-1.7 ms. Rigid bodies vetoed ("weeks, high risk, breaks sync"), reaffirmed in INFORME_REALIDAD §6.

5) CONTRATO_MOTOR (pt39): persistent combustion (aux = fuel reserve), Brasa (id 58, lives 8-12 s, reignites), gases with thermal drift + ceiling pockets, patina byte (renderer-only), zone-of-interest reaction sampling 1/2 vs 1/8, persistent event ring with multi-cursor reader, ParticulasFx overlay (4096 ring, 64 births/frame). Budget: <= ~2 ms added, diluvio may reach ~7.5 ms.

6) CONTRATO_VAPOR (pt41): diagnosis that steam rose perfectly vertical and jittered under ceilings; fix = 30% diagonal wander, low-frequency wind hash FromCell(tick>>4, x>>3, y>>3), ceiling escape order (diag-rumbo, diag-contra, lateral ~60%, lateral-contra); half-decay under ceiling kept ("pockets stay mortal"). Crisol water->Steam "hirviendo" branch added. Alambique condensation mechanic exists and was declared untouchable.

7) CONTRATO_TERMICA (pt44): conversion by fronts in Crisol, HeatPlate/ChillStone rebuilt with distance falloff + Newton-style push; targets ARDIENTE 320°C, HELANDO -25°C (was -80); measurable criteria: boil/freeze in <=6 s at 3 cells, <=±10°C at 12 cells; Termometro (G key, 3 probes, °C). Temperature is NOT replicated to multiplayer guests (only mat). ParticulasFx off by default; wet patina off.

8) DISENO_QUIMICA_REAL / INFORME_REALIDAD / VIDA_UTIL / VISION_FINAL / OPINION_GIRO / FAMILIAS / R78: real-name identity table on frozen seed 777002 (limo = "lodo de cantera"), cross recipes (mortar, cement, bottle glass = sand+ash, lye, glaze), loop discover->produce->store->use->scarcity, physical storage (alacena), trueque economy, empty world, TEN THOUSAND YEARS naming, families F1-F8 with fidelity thresholds. FAMILIAS §7 has the verified fire ladder (brasas≈100 -> turba 124 -> ceniza 136 -> carbón 158-185, cap ≈190) and clay thresholds (adobe 180, calcinado 188, cerámica 205), plus the finding that the current prologue has NO thermal transformation reachable and the trade catalogue is a deadlock. R78 proposes the lingote as a block of real cells (caeSolido + cohesion, regla 7), refill containers with an "owner" material, and parks viscosity/game-feel (fluidity agua=4).

Key gaps for the new laboratory: no gravity sediment settling exists (Limo separates thermally, not by decantation; Familias §6.2 flags column densities possibly reversed); no "agua sucia" material; gas pockets half-decay under ceilings (fights steam conduction); temperature not replicated; no time-scaling mentioned anywhere.
